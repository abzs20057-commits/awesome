Progressive Overload

Increase weight or reps each week. The body only changes when forced to adapt.

Compound-First

Heavy compound lifts before isolation. More muscle recruited = more calories burned.

Supersets

Pair antagonist muscles to slash rest time, spike heart rate, and burn more total calories.

High Protein

Protein preserves muscle in a deficit and keeps you full. Non-negotiable.

Phased Intensity

Three escalating phases ensure the body never adapts and fat loss never stalls.

Strategic Cardio

LISS early, HIIT later. Ramp up the metabolic demand as conditioning improves.

Cardio Plan

Strategic Cardio Ramp-Up

Cardio intensity escalates across the 12 weeks to keep the body in fat-burning mode without sacrificing recovery or strength.

Weeks 1 – 4
3 × 20 min LISS
Incline treadmill walk (3.5–4 mph, 10–15% grade), cycling, or easy rowing at conversational pace
Weeks 5 – 8
2 × LISS + 2 × 10 min HIIT
HIIT after Mon & Thu strength sessions. 20s max effort / 10s rest × 20 rounds on bike, rower, or treadmill
Weeks 9 – 12
1 × LISS + 3 × HIIT + optional fasted walks
Optional: 20 min fasted morning cardio (incline walk or light bike) 3× per week before breakfast

Recovery

Muscle is Built at Rest.

Training is the stimulus. Recovery is where the adaptation actually happens. Neglect this and you'll spin your wheels.

😴

Sleep 7–9 Hours

Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Poor sleep elevates cortisol, impairs fat loss, and destroys muscle retention.

💧

Hydrate 3–4L / Day

Even mild dehydration reduces strength by up to 10%. Drink water consistently throughout the day, not just during training.

🔄

Deload Week 6

After week 6, train at 50–60% intensity for one week. Prevents burnout, consolidates strength gains, and refreshes the CNS.

🧘

Manage Stress

High cortisol from chronic stress actively impairs fat loss and breaks down muscle tissue. Treat stress management as training.

🔥

Foam Roll Daily

5–10 min pre and post session. Focus on tight areas: quads, hip flexors, lats, thoracic spine. Improves mobility and blood flow.

🚶

Active Recovery Saturday

Walk, swim, cycle lightly, or do yoga. Keep moving without stressing the body. Boosts circulation and clears metabolic waste.

12-Week Milestones

Weeks 1 – 2
Establishing habits, tracking nutrition, dialling in form on compound lifts.
Weeks 3 – 4
Noticeable increase in endurance and session intensity. Weights going up.
Weeks 5 – 6
First visible fat loss, muscle definition starting to emerge. Strength gains on main lifts.
Weeks 7 – 8
Significant physique change. Muscles more defined. Waist measurement down noticeably.
Weeks 9 – 10
Conditioning peaks. Higher caloric burn per session. Visible vascularity and striations appearing.
Weeks 11 – 12
Leanest you have ever been. Strength gains preserved. Time to take your after photo.
