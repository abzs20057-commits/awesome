# 12-Week Weightlifting Cut Program: Get Ripped

A structured program combining strength training and metabolic conditioning to cut fat, build muscle, and get shredded.

---

## Program Overview

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Duration | 12 weeks |
| Training days | 5 days/week |
| Session length | 60–75 minutes |
| Goal | Fat loss + muscle retention/growth |
| Split | Upper/Lower + Full-body metabolic |

### Principles

- **Progressive overload** — increase weight or reps each week
- **Caloric deficit** — 300–500 kcal below TDEE
- **High protein** — 0.8–1g per lb of bodyweight daily
- **Compound-first** — heavy compounds before isolation
- **Supersets** — pair antagonist muscles to cut rest time and burn more calories

---

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4: Foundation & Conditioning

**Rest between sets:** 60–90 seconds compounds, 45 seconds isolation

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Notes |
|----------|------|------|-------|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 | 6–8 | Heavy, controlled descent |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 | 10–12 | — |

| Lateral Raises | 3 | 15 | Superset with tricep pushdowns |
| Tricep Pushdowns (cable) | 3 | 15 | Superset with lateral raises |

### Tuesday — Lower A (Squat Focus)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Notes |
|----------|------|------|-------|
| Barbell Back Squat | 4 | 6–8 | Heavy compound |

### Wednesday — Full-Body Metabolic Circuit

3–4 rounds, 30 seconds rest between exercises, 90 seconds rest between rounds.

| Exercise | Duration/Reps |

| Battle Ropes (if available) | 30 seconds |

Finish with 15 minutes of steady-state cardio (bike, treadmill incline walk).

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8: Intensity Increase

Increase weight by 2.5–5% across main lifts. Add one drop set per session on a key isolation movement.

**Changes from Phase 1:**
- Reduce rest to 45–75 seconds (compounds), 30–45 seconds (isolation)
- Add one finisher cardio block (10 min HIIT) to Monday and Thursday sessions
- Increase circuit rounds on Wednesday to 4–5

**HIIT Finisher (10 min):**  
20 seconds on / 10 seconds off × 20 rounds  
Choose: rowing machine, bike, treadmill sprint, or jump rope

### Progressive Overload Targets (Phase 2)

| Lift | Phase 1 Target | Phase 2 Target |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Bench Press | 70–80% 1RM | 75–85% 1RM |
| Squat | 70–80% 1RM | 75–85% 1RM |
| Deadlift | 70–80% 1RM | 80–90% 1RM |
| Pull-ups | Bodyweight | Bodyweight +5–10 kg |

---

## Phase 3 — Weeks 9–12: Peak & Definition

Highest intensity phase. Caloric deficit can tighten to 500 kcal below TDEE. Protein stays high.

**Changes from Phase 2:**
- Add supersets to ALL isolation exercises
- Wednesday circuit becomes an AMRAP (as many rounds as possible in 20 minutes)
- Add 20 minutes fasted morning cardio (optional, 3×/week) — incline walk or bike
- Rest drops to 30–60 seconds compounds, 20–30 seconds isolation

### Sample Superset Pairings (Phase 3)

| Pair | Exercise A | Exercise B |
|------|------------|------------|
| Chest/Back | Incline DB Press | Seated Cable Row |
| Shoulders/Rear Delt | Lateral Raise | Rear Delt Fly |
| Biceps/Triceps | Barbell Curl | Skull Crushers |
| Quads/Hamstrings | Leg Extension | Leg Curl |

## Cardio Plan

| Week | Cardio |
|------|--------|
| 1–4 | 3× 20 min LISS (low intensity steady state) |
| 5–8 | 2× LISS + 2× 10 min HIIT |
| 9–12 | 1× LISS + 3× HIIT + optional fasted walks |

## Nutrition Guidelines

### Caloric Targets

| Goal | Multiplier |
|------|------------|
| Maintenance TDEE | Bodyweight (lbs) × 15 |
| Cut calories | TDEE − 300 to 500 kcal |

### Macros

| Macro | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Protein | 0.8–1g per lb bodyweight |
| Carbohydrates | 1.5–2g per lb bodyweight |
| Fat | 0.35–0.5g per lb bodyweight |

### Meal Timing

- **Pre-workout (60–90 min before):** Complex carbs + protein — oats + eggs, rice + chicken
- **Post-workout (within 60 min):** Fast carbs + protein — rice, sweet potato, protein shake
- **Before bed:** Slow-digesting protein — cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, casein shake

### Foods to Prioritize

**Protein:** Chicken breast, turkey, lean beef, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tuna, salmon, whey/casein protein  
**Carbs:** Rice, sweet potato, oats, quinoa, fruit, vegetables  
**Fats:** Avocado, olive oil, almonds, walnuts, whole eggs, fatty fish  
**Avoid:** Alcohol, processed sugar, fried foods, excessive sodium

## Recovery Essentials

- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours. Muscle is built during recovery, not during training.
- **Hydration:** 3–4 litres of water per day minimum.
- **Deload week:** After week 6, take one week at 50–60% intensity to prevent burnout.
- **Foam rolling:** 5–10 min pre and post session on tight areas.
- **Stress management:** High cortisol from stress impairs fat loss and muscle retention.

---

## Progress Tracking

Track these weekly:

- Bodyweight (same time, same conditions — morning, after bathroom)
- Waist measurement at navel
- Key lift numbers (bench, squat, deadlift, pull-up)
- Weekly progress photos (front, side, back)

**Expected results (consistent effort):**
- 0.5–1 lb of fat lost per week
- Maintain or increase strength on main lifts
- Visible muscle definition improvement by week 6–8

## 12-Week Progress Milestones

| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1–2 | Establishing habits, tracking nutrition, learning the movements |
| 3–4 | Noticeable increase in endurance and workout intensity |
| 5–6 | First visible fat loss, strength gains on compounds |
| 7–8 | Significant physique change, muscles more defined |
| 9–10 | Conditioning peaks, higher caloric burn, visible veins/striations |
| 11–12 | Leanest you've ever been, hold onto strength gains |

> **Note:** Consult a doctor before starting any new exercise or nutrition program. Adjust calories and training volume based on individual response and recovery.
